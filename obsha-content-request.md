# Content request — Obsha Properties

Everything the Nomad Twin Towers site needs from the client in order to ship
the seven additions previewed on `experiment/88-inspired-conversion`.

Four of those seven are pure content problems — the design is built and
working, but it is currently filled with invented placeholder text that cannot
go live (see `PREVIEW-NOTES.md`). This document is the ask that unblocks them.

**How to read the priorities**

- **P1** — the section cannot exist at all without it. Without these we delete
  the section rather than ship it.
- **P2** — the section works without it, but is significantly weaker.
- **P3** — nice to have; we can launch and add later.

Also folded in at the end: the four items still outstanding from the original
brief, so this is a single list rather than two.

---

## 1. Build credibility — for "The Standard" section (P1)

This is the most valuable thing on the list. It answers the question every
off-plan buyer in Nairobi is actually asking — *will this get built, and built
properly* — and it's the one thing the competing sites lean on hardest.

Everything here is a factual claim about regulatory status, so we will publish
**only** what can be evidenced. Copies of documents are ideal; we'll transcribe
the details and won't publish the documents themselves.

**P1 — cannot ship the section without these**

1. **NCA registration** of the main contractor — the registration number and
   the registered company name. (A photo of the certificate is fine.)
2. **Nairobi County development approval** — the approval reference number and
   date of issue.
3. **Land tenure**: is the plot freehold or leasehold? If leasehold, the
   unexpired term. Who holds the mother title?
4. **What buyers actually receive at handover** — sectional title? A share in a
   management company? Something else? This is the single most common question
   from diaspora buyers and we currently cannot answer it.

**P2 — makes the section genuinely persuasive**

5. **Project team**, by name and firm: architect, structural engineer,
   quantity surveyor, main contractor. Where any of them have completed a
   building we can name, that's worth more than the credential itself.
6. **Design standards actually used** — which building code the structure is
   designed to, and which fire standard the building is certified against.
   Please don't guess: if the answer is "Kenyan building code", say that. We
   would much rather publish something modest and true.
7. **Are deposits protected?** Escrow, a project account, a bank guarantee, or
   nothing formal? If there's no protection scheme we simply won't mention it —
   but we need to know either way, because we will otherwise be asked.
8. **Insurance** — contractor's all-risk / professional indemnity in place?

**P3**

9. Any third-party finance on the project (a named bank lending against it is
   independent validation and worth saying).

## 2. Testimonials and social proof — for the "Voices" section (P1)

The site currently has **no social proof of any kind**. The competing site runs
five named quotes, and the most persuasive one is attributed to *"Kenyan
Diaspora (U.S.A), Owner of Whole Floor"* — the role does more work than the
words. We would like four.

For each person, we need:

- Their name, or initials if they'd rather (initials plus a real role still
  works — "S.M., Kenyan diaspora, UK" is credible).
- **Their role or their stake** — this is the part that matters. "Bought a two
  bedroom, floor 14" or "Partner, [law firm]" is worth more than any adjective.
- Two to four sentences in their own words. Unpolished is better than polished.
- Written permission to publish the quote and attribution.
- A photograph only if they're comfortable — the design works without one.

**Who to ask, in order of usefulness:**

1. Anyone who has already reserved or bought a unit — especially a diaspora
   buyer, and especially anyone who bought more than one.
2. The advocate handling the sale agreements, on the transaction process.
3. The architect or main contractor, on the build.
4. A property professional who has visited the site — an agent, a valuer, a
   portal or agency principal.

If nobody will go on record yet, tell us — an honest empty section is better
than a fabricated full one, and we'll drop it until there's something real.

## 3. Construction progress — for the "Progress" section (P1)

Renders sell the dream; progress photos close the sale. Right now the site
shows only renders, so a sceptical buyer has no way to tell whether anything
has been built at all.

**P1**

1. **Current stage of construction, in plain terms** — what is physically
   complete today (e.g. "basements and podium complete, structure at level 8").
2. **Six to ten dated site photographs**, oldest to newest, ideally one every
   few months back to groundbreaking. Phone photos are completely fine — they
   are *more* convincing than professional ones. We need the month each was
   taken.
3. **Confirmed target handover date.** The price list doesn't state one and the
   site currently shows a guess we'd rather remove than publish. (Brief item 3b.)

**P2**

4. Is there a **site camera**, or could one be put up? A live or
   updated-daily view is the strongest version of this section — it's what
   88 Nairobi puts in its main navigation.
5. Are you willing to commit to a **monthly photo update**? If yes we'll design
   it in and say so on the page; if not we'll word it as an occasional update
   rather than a promise the site can't keep.

**P3**

6. The construction programme, so we can show honest stage-by-stage percentages
   rather than one overall number.

## 4. The developer's story — for "The Dream" section (P2)

The site currently credits "Obsha Properties" in the footer and nothing more.
The buyer has no idea who is behind the building.

1. **Founder's name and title**, and whether they're happy to be named on the
   site and quoted.
2. **When Obsha Properties was founded.**
3. **Previous completed developments** — names, locations, years, and unit
   counts. Even two or three is enough. This is track record, and it's the
   second-strongest trust signal after the approvals.
4. **Why this building** — two or three sentences from the founder on what they
   set out to do differently with Nomad. We can draft this from a ten-minute
   phone call if writing it is a chore; we'd just need it approved.
5. **A photograph of the founder**, if they're willing. If not, the section
   works without one — we won't substitute a stock portrait.
6. **A logo for Obsha Properties**, if one exists (brief item 7).

## 5. Photography and location (P2)

1. **A wide photograph of the Westlands skyline** that includes the building's
   plot — for the locator band. A phone photo from a nearby rooftop is fine.
   We currently stand this in with an aerial render.
2. **The exact plot location** — a pin, a plus code, or coordinates — so the
   map is accurate. (Brief item 5, still open.)
3. Confirmation of whether the **restaurant** shown in the renders is the
   6th-floor "Alacat Hotel Restaurant" from the price list or a separate
   amenity-floor space, so we can name it correctly. (Brief item 12.)
4. A view on `pool-closeup.jpg` — it shows a resident holding what reads as a
   cocktail glass. Given the masjid on the 4th floor, is that image on-brand,
   or should we swap it? (Brief, Open questions.)

## 6. Sales and commercial (P1)

1. **Do you want the "call you back within one business day" promise on the
   site?** The new enquiry bar makes it, and it's a real commitment from the
   sales team. If a day isn't realistic, tell us what is — we'll match the copy
   to reality. If you'd rather not commit, we'll remove the line.
2. **The real sales email address.** `sales@nomadtwintowers.com` is still our
   guess; only the domain half is confirmed. (Brief item 2.)
3. **Is `nomadtwintowers.com` already registered to you**, or does it still need
   buying? A `.com` is a different purchase route from the `.co.ke` we costed
   earlier, so this changes the recommendation. (Brief item 8.)
4. **The floor-count discrepancy in your own price list**: the apartment table
   says 18 total floors, while the Project Overview on the same document says
   residential runs 7th–18th, which is twelve floors. Which is right? We've
   removed the storey count from the site rather than guess. (Brief item 3a.)
5. **Payment plan terms** — reservation fee, deposit percentage, instalment
   schedule, handover payment. We removed the previous figures because they
   couldn't be verified; the Ownership section is currently non-numeric and
   noticeably vaguer than competitors'.
6. **Do you want the tier names?** We've mocked up "Signature" and "Signature
   Plus" sitting above "One Bedroom" and "Two Bedroom". It's purely a
   marketing device — happy to drop it, keep it, or use names of your choosing.
