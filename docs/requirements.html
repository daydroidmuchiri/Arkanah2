What we need from Obsha Properties

The site is built and live in preview. What's missing isn't design — it's four blocks of content only you can provide. This is the whole list, in priority order, with what happens to each section if it doesn't arrive.

Please read the preview with this in mind. Four sections of the preview site — The Standard, Voices, Progress and The Dream — are currently filled with invented placeholder text so you can see the layout working. The consultant names, registration numbers, testimonials, construction dates and founder story on that page are not real and were not supplied by you. They are there purely to show the shape of each section, and none of it will go live.

1. Build credibility

For "The Standard" section — the one that answers the question every off-plan buyer is actually asking.

This is the most valuable thing on the list. Competing developments lean on this harder than on anything else, and Nomad currently says nothing about it at all.

Everything here is a factual claim about regulatory status, so we will publish only what can be evidenced. Photos of certificates are perfect — we'll transcribe the details and won't publish the documents themselves.

Cannot ship the section without these

  1. P1NCA registration of the main contractor — the registration number and registered company name.
  2. P1Nairobi County development approval — the reference number and date of issue.
  3. P1Land tenure — freehold or leasehold? If leasehold, the unexpired term. Who holds the mother title?
  4. P1What buyers actually receive at handover — sectional title, a share in a management company, or something else? This is the most common question from diaspora buyers and we currently cannot answer it.

Makes the section genuinely persuasive

  1. P2Project team by name and firm — architect, structural engineer, quantity surveyor, main contractor. Where any of them has completed a building we can name, that's worth more than the credential itself.
  2. P2Design standards actually used — which building code the structure is designed to, and which fire standard it's certified against. Please don't guess: if the answer is "the Kenyan building code", say that. We would much rather publish something modest and true.
  3. P2Are deposits protected? Escrow, a project account, a bank guarantee, or nothing formal? If there's no protection scheme we simply won't mention it — but we need to know either way, because buyers will ask.
  4. P2Insurance — contractor's all-risk and professional indemnity in place?
  5. P3Any third-party finance on the project. A named bank lending against it is independent validation, and worth saying.

2. Testimonials

For the "Voices" section. The site currently has no social proof of any kind.

We'd like four. The most persuasive testimonial on any competing site in this market is attributed simply to "Kenyan Diaspora (U.S.A), Owner of Whole Floor" — the role does more work than the words.

For each person we need

  • Their name, or initials if they'd prefer. Initials plus a real role still works.
  • Their role or their stake — this is the part that matters. "Bought a two bedroom, floor 14" or "Partner, [law firm]" beats any adjective.
  • Two to four sentences in their own words. Unpolished is better than polished.
  • Written permission to publish the quote and the attribution.
  • A photograph only if they're comfortable. The design works without one.

Who to ask, in order of usefulness

  1. P1Anyone who has already reserved or bought — especially a diaspora buyer, and especially anyone who bought more than one unit.
  2. P2The advocate handling the sale agreements, on the transaction process.
  3. P2The architect or main contractor, on the build.
  4. P3A property professional who has visited the site — an agent, a valuer, an agency principal.

If nobody will go on record yet, please just tell us. An honest empty section is better than a fabricated full one, and we'll drop it until there's something real.

3. Construction progress

For the "Progress" section. Renders sell the dream; progress photos close the sale.

Right now the site shows only renders, so a sceptical buyer has no way to tell whether anything has been built at all.

  1. P1Current stage of construction in plain terms — what is physically complete today, e.g. "basements and podium complete, structure at level 8".
  2. P1Six to ten dated site photographs, oldest to newest, ideally one every few months back to groundbreaking. Phone photos are completely fine — they are more convincing than professional ones. We need the month each was taken.
  3. P1Confirmed target handover date. The price list doesn't state one, and the site currently shows a guess that we would rather remove than publish.
  4. P2Is there a site camera, or could one be put up? A live or daily-updated view is the strongest version of this section.
  5. P2Will you commit to a monthly photo update? If yes, we'll design it in and say so on the page. If not, we'll word it as an occasional update rather than a promise the site can't keep.
  6. P3The construction programme, so we can show honest stage-by-stage percentages rather than one overall number.

4. The developer's story

For "The Dream" section. The site currently credits "Obsha Properties" in the footer and nothing more.

  1. P2Founder's name and title, and whether they're happy to be named and quoted on the site.
  2. P2When Obsha Properties was founded.
  3. P1Previous completed developments — names, locations, years, unit counts. Even two or three is enough. This is track record, and it is the second-strongest trust signal after the approvals.
  4. P2Why this building — two or three sentences from the founder on what they set out to do differently with Nomad. We can draft this from a ten-minute phone call if writing it is a chore; we'd just need it approved.
  5. P3A photograph of the founder, if they're willing. If not, the section works without one — we will not substitute a stock portrait.
  6. P2An Obsha Properties logo, if one exists.

5. Photography and location

  1. P2A wide photograph of the Westlands skyline that includes the plot, for the locator band. A phone photo from a nearby rooftop is fine. We currently stand this in with an aerial render.
  2. P1The exact plot location — a pin, a plus code, or coordinates — so the map is accurate. Still outstanding from the original brief.
  3. P2Is the restaurant in the renders the 6th-floor "Alacat Hotel Restaurant" from your price list, or a separate amenity-floor space? We've kept the name generic until this is confirmed.
  4. P2A view on the rooftop pool image — it shows a resident holding what reads as a cocktail glass. Given the masjid on the 4th floor, is that image on-brand, or should we swap it?

6. Sales and commercial

  1. P1Do you want the "we'll call you back within one business day" promise on the site? The new enquiry bar makes it, and it's a real commitment from the sales team. If a day isn't realistic, tell us what is and we'll match the copy to reality. If you'd rather not commit, we'll remove the line.
  2. P1The real sales email address. sales@nomadtwintowers.com is still our guess — only the domain half is confirmed.
  3. P1Is nomadtwintowers.com already registered to you, or does it still need buying? A .com is a different purchase route from the .co.ke we costed earlier.
  4. P1A discrepancy in your own price list. The apartment table says 18 total floors, while the Project Overview on the same document says residential runs 7th–18th, which is twelve floors. Which is right? We've removed the storey count from the site rather than guess.
  5. P1Payment plan terms — reservation fee, deposit percentage, instalment schedule, handover payment. We removed the previous figures because they couldn't be verified, so the Ownership section is currently non-numeric and noticeably vaguer than competitors'.
  6. P3Do you want the tier names? The preview shows "Signature" and "Signature Plus" sitting above "One Bedroom" and "Two Bedroom". It's purely a marketing device — happy to drop it, keep it, or use names of your choosing.
