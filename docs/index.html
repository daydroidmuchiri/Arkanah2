The Vision

Built for the way Eastleigh works.

Nomad Twin Towers is a landmark mixed-use address in the heart of Eastleigh's business district — 334 shops and a 1,701 sqm supermarket at street level, 252 luxury residences above, and hotel suites crowning the towers. Every residence is designed for natural light, with floor-to-ceiling glazing throughout.

Eastleigh trades from first light, and the towers are built for that rhythm: a home minutes from Business Bay Square and three kilometres from the CBD, above a retail podium that works as hard as the district around it. Take a residence, take a shop — or hold both.
