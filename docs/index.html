The Vision

Quiet luxury, engineered for living.

Nomad Twin Towers is a landmark mixed-use address moments from the Westlands business district — a boutique retail podium and supermarket at street level, luxury residences above, and hotel suites crowning the towers. Every residence is designed for natural light, with floor-to-ceiling glazing throughout.

Designed around the rhythms of modern Nairobi, the towers pair serene private homes with shared amenity space and a retail podium below — so the city, and everything it offers, is always close, and never intrusive.

The Dream

One address, seventeen years in the making.

Ahmed Obsha founded Obsha Properties in 2009 with a single conviction: that Nairobi deserved buildings finished to the standard its people already lived to. Four completed developments later, Nomad Twin Towers is the one he had been building toward — a mixed-use address where the retail, the residences and the hotel were drawn as one building rather than stacked as three.

We were not trying to build the tallest thing in Westlands. We were trying to build the one people still want to live in twenty years from now.

Ahmed Obsha · Founder, Obsha Properties

The Standard

Built to be inspected, not just admired.

Every claim on this page is backed by a drawing, an approval or a certificate — and we'll show you all three before you sign anything.

Structure

Reinforced concrete frame designed to Eurocode 8 seismic provisions for the Nairobi region, with an independent structural check before each pour.

  • Structural EngineerKanyi & Partners Consulting
  • Design CodeEurocode 2 / Eurocode 8
  • Main ContractorSandhurst Construction Ltd

Safety & Fire

Two protected escape stairs, a sprinklered podium and residential core, addressable detection throughout, and a fire tank sized for full-building suppression.

  • Fire StandardNFPA 101 / BS 9991
  • CertificationNairobi County Fire Dept.
  • Backup PowerDual generators, N+1

Approvals & Title

Full county development approval in place, NCA-registered contractor, and a freehold mother title held by the project's own SPV — buyers receive a registered sectional title on completion.

  • NCA RegistrationNCA/1/2019/04471
  • County ApprovalNCC/DEV/2024/1188
  • TenureFreehold · sectional title

Buyer protection. Deposits are held in a joint escrow account operated by the project advocates and released against certified construction milestones. Ask the sales team for the escrow agreement and the latest engineer's certificate.
